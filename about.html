Welcome to my website! I'm a musician and sound artist who uses computers to shape sound. I'm currently based in Saint-Étienne, France.

I have a background in Computer Music and Music Composition. I am passionate about exploring the creative possibilities of sound and technology, and I am always looking for new ways to push my artistic skills to new heights.

I'm the president of the Cosmonaut Collective, a group of tech artist geeks who explore modern tools to create immersive experiences through which we share our stories about the new cyberpunk world we live in.

Here you can find a detailed portfolio of my work, as well as my creative coding experiments and my thoughts on music, sound, art, and technology. Don't hesitate to reach out to me for collaborations, requests, or simply to chat.
